Overview

1% Club — Luxury Widget is a simple iOS widget app that displays your net worth on your home screen. We are committed to your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

The short version: we collect nothing. No accounts, no sign-ups, no servers.

What We Don't Collect

  • No name, email address, or personal identifiers
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No usage analytics or crash reports sent to us
  • No browsing history or behavioral data
  • No financial account data

Data Stored on Your Device

The only information stored by the app is the net worth amount you choose to enter, along with your selected widget theme and purchase status. This data is stored exclusively in iOS UserDefaults (App Group) on your local device — it never leaves your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

You can delete this data at any time by uninstalling the app.

Third-Party Services

1% Club does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with any third party.

The app offers a one-time in-app purchase. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment information. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchase transactions: apple.com/legal/privacy

Children's Privacy

1% Club does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is not directed at children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
